**IDBERRY began with a voice.** Long before the skincare, Idunnunmi Esan was a hip-hop singer, and the studio crews she worked with said her voice was "sweet like berry." She added "ID", short for Idunnunmi, and ID Berry was born. Today that name belongs to a luxury skincare brand built on the same sweetness: rituals for even tone, clear complexions and a glow that feels like you.

## Who is Idunnunmi "IDBERRY" Esan?

Idunnunmi Esan is the Founder and CEO of IDBERRY Beauty. She is also a Nollywood actress, a filmmaker through Idberry Movie Productions, and a cosmetologist.

She studied Public Administration at the Federal Polytechnic, Kaura Namoda. Her first film role came in 2008, on a production shot in Ibadan. Since then her screen work has included *Idberry Ijogbon* (2015), *Retribution* (2018), *Unavailable* (2019), *Foyinsola* (2021), *Ojiji Mi* and *Asiri Iboji*.

Across music, film and beauty, one thread runs through everything she does: building something of her own. In her words: "I'm an advocate for women building their own empires."

## How did a nickname become a beauty brand?

The story of the name was shared with City People in May 2020. In her hip-hop days, people in the studio kept describing her voice the same way: sweet like berry. Combined with "ID", from Idunnunmi, the nickname stuck. Today it lives on in her film company, Idberry Movie Productions, and in her skincare line.

She describes IDBERRY as "a luxury skincare and cosmetics brand," and the brand carries the warmth of that original nickname into every ritual.

## What does IDBERRY believe about beauty?

For Idunnunmi, skincare is about confidence, not about becoming someone else. "True beauty begins within," she says. "When a woman feels confident, it radiates through her skin, her smile and her energy."

She is equally clear that beauty is only part of the story: "Physical beauty can open doors, but character, intelligence and purpose are what keep those doors open."

And on what the brand stands for: "I believe in enhancing your natural beauty through good skincare, fitness, and self-care — that's what I promote with IDBERRY."

That philosophy shapes the four pillars behind every IDBERRY product:

- **Results you can see.**
- **Rituals, not random products.**
- **Made for melanin-rich skin.**
- **Guidance from a cosmetologist.**

The mission is simple: to help every woman feel confident in her skin through effective, results-driven rituals and honest guidance.

## What does IDBERRY make today?

IDBERRY's rituals are grouped into collections, each with its own character. Snow White is the signature radiance ritual for a luminous, even complexion. Half Cast is a golden-toned glow ritual for warm, honeyed radiance. Caramel Glow is for deep, rich tones that want to stay rich and simply glow. Royal Customized is the most indulgent ritual, tailored to you. Targeted Care offers focused treatments for scars, stretch marks, spots, breakouts and dark knuckles, and Little Ones brings gentle care to kids' skin and hair. There is also a herbal line, including the Pure Herbal Skinglow Black Soap.

As with any skincare, we recommend patch testing new products, wearing sunscreen daily, and seeing a dermatologist for any persistent skin concern.

## Where can you find IDBERRY?

IDBERRY's studio is at 2 Prince Durotoye Street, beside Vitafoam, Iju Road, Agege 100216, open Monday to Friday 9am to 7pm and Saturday 10am to 6pm. The brand delivers nationwide across Nigeria and ships internationally, and offers free skin consultations on WhatsApp.

For entrepreneurs, IDBERRY also offers wholesale pricing and private-label production, so other women can build beauty brands of their own. As Idunnunmi puts it: "My goal is to build empires that last beyond the screen."
