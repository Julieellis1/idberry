The best skincare ritual for melanin-rich skin is a short one you can keep: **cleanse gently, apply one targeted treatment, moisturise, and protect with sunscreen every morning.** Melanin-rich skin is beautifully resilient, but it can also react to irritation by forming dark marks, so the goal is a ritual that is effective without being harsh. Below is the framework we use when we build rituals for clients in our Lagos studio.

## Why does melanin-rich skin need a gentle ritual?

Skin with more melanin tends to respond to inflammation by producing extra pigment. A pimple, a scratch, an over-scrubbed patch or a product that stings can leave a dark mark that lingers for weeks. That is why "more" is rarely better. Harsh scrubs, too many active ingredients at once and skipping moisturiser can all leave skin looking dull and uneven instead of radiant.

A gentle ritual keeps the skin barrier comfortable. When the barrier is healthy, skin holds moisture better, looks smoother and reflects light more evenly, which is what most of us mean when we say we want to glow.

## What are the four steps of a simple ritual?

Think of your ritual as four jobs rather than a long list of products.

1. **Cleanse.** Remove sweat, sunscreen and the day's grime without leaving skin tight. A creamy cleanser or a gentle soap works for most people. If your skin feels squeaky afterwards, your cleanser is too strong.
2. **Treat.** This is where you target your goal: radiance, even tone or clarity. A vitamin C serum in the morning is a popular choice for brightness. Use one treatment at a time so you can tell what is working.
3. **Moisturise.** Even oily skin needs water and lightweight moisture. Lotions and creams help keep the barrier comfortable, and a few drops of oil can seal in hydration on drier areas.
4. **Protect.** Finish every morning with a broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. Reapply when you are outdoors for long stretches.

## What should my morning and evening routine look like?

**Morning:** cleanse, apply your treatment serum, moisturise, then sunscreen. Keep it light so it sits well under makeup and in the heat.

**Evening:** cleanse thoroughly (twice if you wore sunscreen and makeup), then moisturise. Evening is a good time for richer creams or a body oil, because you are not heading out into the sun.

**Weekly:** a gentle exfoliation once or twice a week can help skin look smoother. Go easy. Scrubbing hard or daily is one of the most common causes of irritation and dark patches on deeper skin tones.

Don't forget your body. Elbows, knees and knuckles often get darker and drier than the rest of the skin, so give them the same cleanse, moisturise and protect care.

## How do I introduce a new product safely?

New products are exciting, but adding several at once makes it impossible to know which one your skin loves, or which one it doesn't.

- **Patch test first.** Apply a small amount behind your ear or on your inner arm and wait 24 to 48 hours. Redness, itching, burning or bumps mean that product is not for you.
- **Add one product at a time.** Wait one to two weeks before introducing the next.
- **Start slowly with strong treatments.** Use them every other day at first and build up as your skin settles.
- **Read the label.** Choose products with a clear ingredient list from a brand you can contact with questions.

## How long before I see a difference?

Skin renews itself gradually, so give a consistent ritual four to eight weeks before you judge it. Many people notice skin feeling softer and looking fresher sooner, while evenness takes longer. Take a photo in the same light every two weeks; it is far more reliable than the mirror.

Results vary from person to person and depend on your skin, your environment and how consistently you follow the ritual. If something stings, burns or causes a rash, stop using it. And if breakouts, irritation or dark marks persist, please see a dermatologist. Some concerns need a professional diagnosis, and getting one early saves time and money.

## Where should I start if I feel overwhelmed?

Start with three things: a gentle cleanser, a moisturiser you enjoy using, and a sunscreen you will actually wear every day. Once those feel automatic, add one treatment for your main goal.
